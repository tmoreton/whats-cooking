Questions

Good to know

Do I have to type in my ingredients?

Never. Just take a photo of your fridge, pantry or spice rack and the AI reads the ingredients for you. You can always add or remove items by hand if you want.

Are the recipes real, or made up?

The AI generates original recipes tailored to what you actually have, complete with steps and timings. A confidence check keeps it grounded so it won't invent ingredients it can't see.

Does it handle dietary restrictions?

Yes. Toggle vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free and every suggestion respects your choice.
