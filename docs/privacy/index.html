Privacy policy

Simple, private by design.

What's Cooking? turns photos of your fridge, pantry, and spice rack into recipe suggestions. This policy explains what happens to your data.

Last updated August 26, 2026

Information the app processes

  • Photos you take for a scan. The app resizes them on your device and sends them securely to our backend so an AI model can identify ingredients and create recipes.
  • Preferences and recent results. Dietary settings and recent scan results are stored only on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Technical connection data. Our infrastructure may retain limited request metadata, including an IP address, request identifier, route, status, and timing, in operational and security logs for up to 14 days.

The app has no account system and does not request your name, email address, contacts, or precise location.

How photos are used

Photos are transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our backend on Amazon Web Services in the United States. They are processed transiently to return ingredient and recipe results and are not intentionally retained after that real-time request.

We do not sell photos, use them for advertising, or allow third parties to use them for their own marketing.

Camera access

The app requests camera access only so you can photograph food and ingredients. It does not access your photo library or record audio.

Retention and your choices

  • Clear recent results from inside the app at any time.
  • Deleting the app removes its on-device preferences and recent results.
  • Operational and security logs are automatically deleted after no more than 14 days.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

What's Cooking? contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites and do not sell personal data.

Service providers

Amazon Web Services provides the infrastructure that processes scan requests on our behalf. We do not share app data with third parties for their own purposes.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact and privacy requests

For a privacy question or request, contact the developer through GitHub support or visit the developer profile.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top of this page will show the latest revision.
