App support

How can we help?

Find quick answers below or contact the developer. When reporting a problem, include your device model, iOS version, and what happened just before the issue.

💬

Contact the developer

What's Cooking? is maintained by @tmoreton on GitHub. Email tmoreton89@gmail.com or open a support issue for app problems, feedback, and feature requests.

Open a support issue

The camera does not open

Open iOS Settings, find What's Cooking?, and make sure Camera access is enabled. Return to the app and start the scan again.

A scan does not finish

Confirm that the device has an internet connection, then try again with one clear photo. Photos are sent securely for real-time ingredient analysis, so scanning cannot complete while offline.

Delete recent results

Use the clear option in the app's recent scans view. You can also remove all locally stored app data by deleting the app from the device.

Privacy questions

Read the privacy policy for details about photo processing, local storage, and short-lived operational logs.
