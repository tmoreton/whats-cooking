Turn what you already have into something worth cooking.

What's Cooking uses AI vision to identify ingredients from photos of your fridge, pantry, and spice rack. It then creates practical recipe ideas based on the food you already have.

HOW IT WORKS

• Snap one or more photos of your ingredients
• Choose vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free preferences
• Let AI identify what's available
• Get recipes ranked by what you can make
• See missing ingredients at a glance
• Follow clear, step-by-step directions

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT

• Spend less time deciding what to cook
• Make better use of groceries already at home
• Reduce unnecessary food waste
• Scan your fridge, pantry, and spices in one session
• Revisit recent scans and share recipes

Whether you're planning dinner, using up leftovers, or looking for inspiration, What's Cooking turns a quick photo into your next meal.
